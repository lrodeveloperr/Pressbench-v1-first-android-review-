# PressBench Commercial Plan

The planned future Android ad-free subscription reference price is **US$9.99 per month**.

This is an internal planning value only. The `1.0.0-closed-v16-native` closed-test build does not display or sell this subscription and does not include Google Play Billing. Before offering it, implement and test Play Billing, create the Play subscription/base plan, retrieve the user's localized price from Google Play, and synchronize the app, listing and legal disclosures.
